## Hard boundaries

- Preserve the complete public inventory and registry/Markdown parity.
- Use pinned, source-owned public evidence for displayed claims; generated reports are secondary.
- Exactly two private systems may be described publicly: DAD at `https://github.com/lowelltwong-alt/Digital-Assett-Directory` and Albert Mock Trial at `https://github.com/lowelltwong-alt/Albert-Trial-Simulation-System`. Both remain access-controlled; permission for one does not grant the other. DAD is the sole structured private evidence route in the machine registry. Albert may appear only in Markdown with the bounded source-relative review paths and non-claims in `AI_FRONT_DOOR.md`; the URL is an access-request entry, and the held workbench candidate must not be represented as present on its current default branch. Do not add it to public evidence registries before a separately reviewed public release.
- Do not expose local absolute paths, private payloads, real matters, a deeper Albert core, or any other private repository, branch, artifact, test result, source record, or capability claim.
- Do not claim production deployment, real-data validation, autonomous execution, or LLM probabilistic evaluation.
- The deterministic validation spine is Intake, Logos Scripture Graph, Orchestrator, and Skills Registry. Orphan Radar alone has bounded stochastic calibration for review-candidate ranking.
- Mesh/swarm terms apply only to bounded development/workflow governance in Logos and DAD, never to Albert or to a continuously operating autonomous product swarm.

Run `python scripts/validate_profile_package.py` after updates. Run the privacy-boundary scan before release preparation.
