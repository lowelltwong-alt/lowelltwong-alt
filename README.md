**Logos-first, whole-portfolio review**

Logos is a Scripture-grounded AI governance and enablement architecture. It begins with Scripture as the highest authority layer and makes the chain from source → doctrinal interpretation → institutional commitments → governance → decision rights → workflow explicit, contestable, and auditable. Its multi-repository design separates governance authority, Scripture data, boundary literature, and doctrine genealogy so AI can support governed inquiry without becoming theological authority.

Logos adapts that decision pattern as **LAIRCA** by adding an explicit Logos-grounding layer, keeping theological authority and institutional commitments upstream of AI-supported decisions.

## Boundaries

- Public examples use synthetic or public-source material; they do not establish real-data validation.
- Public maturity varies by repository; no production deployment is claimed here.
- “Bounded” means human-accountable review and constrained authority, not an autonomous product swarm.
- Public evidence does not claim LLM-based probabilistic evaluation. Deterministic validation is the spine; only Orphan Radar uses bounded stochastic calibration for candidate ranking.
